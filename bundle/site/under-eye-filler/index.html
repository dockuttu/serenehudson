Look rested, even when you’re not

Hollows and shadows under the eyes can make you look tired no matter how much sleep you get. Under-eye (tear trough) filler gently restores volume in this delicate area for a smoother, brighter look.

The under-eye is one of the most delicate areas to treat, which is exactly why it belongs in experienced physician hands. At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, our board-certified physicians assess whether you’re a good candidate and treat conservatively for natural, refreshed results. Results are visible quickly and typically last several months to a year; individual results vary.

After Your Visit

Aftercare

Love your results, protect your investment — a few simple steps help minimize swelling and bruising.

First 24 Hours

  • Avoid touching, rubbing, or massaging the area.
  • Apply ice packs gently for swelling (10 min on / off).
  • Stay hydrated and sleep with your head elevated.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise.
  • Avoid alcohol and excessive salt.
  • Avoid excessive heat — saunas, hot tubs, steam rooms, tanning beds.

First 48 Hours

  • Avoid facials, massage, microneedling, chemical peels, and laser treatments.
  • Avoid pressure on the area — face-down sleeping, tight goggles, excessive facial massage.

Lips Specifically

  • Expect more swelling the next morning.
  • Use a straw minimally for the first day.
  • Small lumps are often swelling and usually soften over 1–2 weeks.
