Rebuild volume, naturally, over time

Sculptra isn’t a traditional filler — instead of simply adding volume, it uses poly-L-lactic acid to prompt your skin to produce its own collagen, restoring fullness gradually and subtly.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, Sculptra is a favorite for patients who want a natural refresh that doesn’t look ‘done.’ Because results build over a series of sessions as new collagen forms, the change is beautifully gradual — and it can last up to a couple of years. Treatment is always physician-led, and individual results vary.

After Your Visit

Aftercare

Following these instructions helps ensure beautiful, natural-looking results.

First 24–48 Hours

  • Mild swelling, redness, tenderness, or bruising at the injection sites is normal.
  • Avoid touching, massaging, or applying pressure to the area.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise, heat, and alcohol.
  • Stay hydrated.

Massage — the 5-5-5 Rule

  • Massage the treated areas 5 minutes, 5 times a day, for 5 days.
  • Use firm pressure.
  • This helps Sculptra distribute evenly and supports optimal results.

Skin Care & Sun

  • Use a gentle cleanser and moisturizer.
  • Avoid active ingredients (retinol, acids, exfoliants) for 24–48 hours.
  • Wear SPF 30+ daily and avoid direct sun for at least 24 hours.
  • You may wear makeup after 24 hours.

Good to Know

Sculptra FAQ

Traditional fillers add volume instantly. Sculptra works gradually by stimulating your own collagen, for a subtle, natural change over time.

Most people need a series of sessions spaced over a few months. We’ll design a plan based on your goals.

Because it builds your own collagen, Sculptra results can last up to a couple of years — longer than many traditional fillers.

Results appear gradually over weeks to months, so the change looks natural rather than sudden.

Mild swelling or bruising is possible. We’ll also show you a simple massage technique to do at home after treatment.
