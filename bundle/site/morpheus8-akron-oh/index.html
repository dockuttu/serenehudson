Tightening that reaches the layer that matters

Ordinary microneedling injures the surface and lets healing do the rest. Morpheus8 adds radiofrequency energy delivered at a controlled depth, so the remodeling happens in the deeper dermis and subdermal fat where laxity actually lives.

That depth control is what makes it versatile. Set shallow, it addresses texture, pores and acne scarring. Set deeper, it contracts the fibrous layer under the skin — which is why it is used along the jawline and under the chin, and why it works on the abdomen and knees as well as the face.

A treatment is $800, and a series of three is $2,100. Three is the usual recommendation, spaced about a month apart, with results continuing to build for several months afterward as collagen remodels. MorpheusV for remodeling is $800, and Morpheus8 for hyperhidrosis is $600.

Downtime is modest — typically a sunburn-like redness and mild grid-pattern texture for two to four days. Akron patients commonly book Friday afternoons for that reason.
