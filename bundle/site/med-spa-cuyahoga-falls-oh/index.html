A physician practice, not a franchise, just north of the Falls

Cuyahoga Falls patients tell us the same thing: they searched “botox fillers cuyahoga falls” and found chains and part-time injectors. They wanted a doctor. We’re that practice, 15 minutes up Route 8 in downtown Hudson.

Serene Med Spa is owned by Dr. Shweta Arora and Dr. Robin Arora, both board-certified physicians. There is no separate “injector” — the doctor who examines your face is the doctor who treats it. We’re an Allergan Platinum partner (top tier for Botox, Juvéderm, SKINVIVE and Kybella volume), an Ultherapy PRIME provider and an Alma and InMode practice, so the full range of treatments is under one roof.

Pricing is simple and public: Botox $11/unit, Juvéderm and Restylane $500/syringe, Morpheus8 $800 (three sessions $2,100), Kybella $600/vial, HydraFacial $150. See the full price list. Consultations are free, and if you’re a candidate we can usually treat you at that first visit.

From Front Street, Portage Trail or State Road the drive is 15–18 minutes: Route 8 north to Route 303, east two minutes into Hudson, park at the door. Patients from Northampton and the Blossom / Cuyahoga Valley side come up Akron-Peninsula Rd or Route 8 the same way.
