The med spa at the other end of Route 303

If you live in Aurora, Streetsboro or Mantua, you already know Route 303. Follow it west into Hudson and you’ll find Serene at 50 W Streetsboro St — the practice Portage County patients choose for physician injectors and honest pricing.

Serene Med Spa is owned by Dr. Shweta Arora and Dr. Robin Arora, both board-certified physicians. They do every consultation and every injection. We’re an Allergan Platinum partner, an Ultherapy PRIME provider, and an Alma and InMode practice, which means the full menu is here: Botox, the whole Juvéderm family, SKINVIVE, Kybella, Morpheus8, Opus Plasma, Soprano laser hair removal, HydraFacial, EMSCULPT NEO and physician-supervised weight loss.

Every price is public — Botox $11/unit, fillers $500/syringe, HydraFacial $150, Morpheus8 $800 — on our price list. Consultations are free and can become treatment the same day.

From Aurora it’s about 15 minutes: Route 43 south to Route 303, then west into Hudson. From Streetsboro it’s a straight 10-minute run west on 303 (past the Route 8 interchange, into downtown). Mantua and Garrettsville patients follow Route 82 or 303 west the same way. Free parking is at the door.

Your First Visit

What to expect when you drive in from Aurora

Book online or call

Pick a free consultation or go straight to a treatment on our online calendar. Same-week openings are common; tell us if you’re coming from out of town and we’ll stack your visits.

Park at the door

We’re at 50 W Streetsboro St (Route 303) in downtown Hudson with free parking right outside Suite 2 — no garage, no meters.

Meet your physician

Dr. Shweta Arora or Dr. Robin Arora reviews your goals, your skin and your history, then builds a plan around your budget. Most consults take 20–30 minutes.

Treat the same day

Botox, filler, SKINVIVE, facials and most laser treatments can be done at the consult if you’re a candidate, so one drive covers both.
