Glow, refreshed and radiant

A laser facial uses gentle laser energy to brighten tone, refine texture, and stimulate your skin’s natural renewal — delivering a healthy, radiant glow with minimal downtime.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, a laser facial is a wonderful way to refresh dull, uneven skin and keep your complexion looking its best. It suits a range of concerns, from redness and blotchiness to overall radiance, and pairs beautifully with the rest of your skincare plan. Treatment is physician-supervised, and individual results vary.
