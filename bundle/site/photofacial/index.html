Even, clear, radiant skin

A photofacial uses broadband (BBL) or intense pulsed light (IPL) to target the excess pigment and redness that make skin look aged or blotchy — clearing sun spots and calming redness for a brighter, more even complexion.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, photofacials are a favorite for sun-damaged and redness-prone skin. The light gently targets brown spots and broken-looking capillaries, which fade as your skin clears them over the following days. There’s little downtime, a series delivers the best results, and individual results vary.

After Your Visit

Aftercare

Proper aftercare protects your skin, reduces irritation, and maximizes your results.

First 24–48 Hours

  • Expect redness, warmth, and mild swelling.
  • Use a gentle cleanser and moisturizer.
  • Avoid touching or scratching treated areas.

Pigmented Spots — “Coffee Grounds”

  • Brown spots may darken temporarily before flaking off naturally.
  • Do NOT pick or scrub.

Avoid Several Days

  • Strenuous exercise, sweating, saunas, hot tubs, steam rooms, and pools.
  • Retinoids/Retin-A, exfoliants, glycolic acids, scrubs, and harsh actives until healed.
  • Makeup for 24 hours if irritated (mineral preferred); wear SPF 30+ and avoid tanning beds or self-tanner.
