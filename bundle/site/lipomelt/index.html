Effortless, relaxing body support

Lipomelt uses specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light, delivered through comfortable pads, to support your body-contouring goals — all while you simply relax.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, Lipomelt is a gentle, non-invasive option that complements a healthy diet, exercise, and your other body treatments. It’s completely comfortable with no downtime. Lipomelt supports body-contouring goals as part of a healthy lifestyle rather than acting as a weight-loss treatment on its own; a series works best and individual results vary.
