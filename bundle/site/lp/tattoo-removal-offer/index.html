How it works

Consultation

We look at the ink colors, depth, age and your skin, then give you a size price and a realistic plan for full removal or cover-up fading.

Short sessions

The laser breaks ink into tiny particles. Most sessions take just minutes.

Your body does the rest

Your immune system clears the ink between visits, so sessions are spaced about 6–8 weeks apart. Each one fades it further.

Questions

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the ink, colors, age of the tattoo and where it is on the body. Many tattoos need several sessions, and some need more. We’ll give you an estimate at your consultation. Results vary.

Does it hurt?

Most people compare it to a rubber-band snap. Sessions are short, and we use cooling to keep you comfortable.

Can you just lighten it for a cover-up?

Yes. Fading usually takes fewer sessions than full removal and gives your artist a cleaner canvas.

Are some colors harder to remove?

Black and dark blue usually respond best. Some colors, like light green, light blue and yellow, can take more sessions. We’ll set expectations at your consultation.

What’s aftercare like?

Expect redness and swelling for a few days, sometimes blisters. Keep it clean and covered, and avoid sun on the area. We’ll give you written aftercare.
