Questions

How many sessions will I need?

Most people need about 6, spaced 4–6 weeks apart (face areas a bit sooner). Results vary with hair color, thickness and hormones, and some people need occasional touch-ups.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe a quick snap or warm sensation. Sessions are short, and cooling keeps them comfortable.

How do I prepare?

Shave the area the day before. Skip waxing, plucking and threading for 4 weeks before your session (they remove the root the laser targets). Avoid sun and self-tanner for 2 weeks.

Is it right for my skin?

We match the laser setting to your skin and hair type at your consultation. If laser isn’t a good fit for you, we’ll tell you.
