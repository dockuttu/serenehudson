Rethink that ink

Modern picosecond (PICO) lasers shatter tattoo ink into tiny particles your body clears naturally — fading unwanted tattoos more effectively and with fewer sessions than older lasers.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, we use advanced PICO technology to remove or fade tattoos of various sizes and colors. Because your body clears the ink gradually, removal happens over a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart. The number of sessions depends on the tattoo’s size, age, colors, and ink depth; individual results vary.

After Your Visit

Aftercare

Proper aftercare is essential for safe healing, minimizing complications, and achieving the best results.

First 24–48 Hours

  • Keep the treated area clean and dry.
  • Apply a thin layer of healing ointment if instructed.
  • Cover with a clean bandage if needed.
  • Use cool compresses for comfort or swelling.
  • Avoid touching, scratching, or picking.

Important Healing

  • Do NOT pop blisters.
  • Do NOT pick scabs.
  • Allow the skin to heal naturally to reduce scarring risk.
  • Avoid tight or irritating clothing over the area.

Avoid Until Healed

  • Swimming pools, hot tubs, and saunas.
  • Excessive sweating.
  • Exfoliants and harsh skincare products.
  • Direct sun — wear SPF 30+ once healed; no tanning beds or self-tanner.
