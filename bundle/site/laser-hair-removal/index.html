Smoother skin, far less upkeep

Laser hair removal targets the follicle to reduce unwanted hair over a series of sessions — so you can spend less time shaving and waxing, and more time enjoying smooth skin.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, we treat both small and large areas, from the upper lip to the legs and back. Because hair grows in cycles, a series of treatments spaced a few weeks apart gives the best, longest-lasting results. Your plan is guided by our physicians and tailored to your skin and hair; individual results vary.

After Your Visit

Aftercare

Proper aftercare reduces irritation and protects your skin while it heals for the best, longest-lasting results.

First 24–48 Hours

  • Avoid excessive heat — hot tubs, saunas, steam rooms, very hot showers.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise or heavy sweating.
  • Wear loose, breathable clothing over treated areas.
  • Don’t scratch, pick, or exfoliate the skin.
  • Avoid perfumed lotions or irritating products.

Sun & Skin Care

  • Avoid direct sun for 1–2 weeks; wear SPF 30+ daily.
  • No tanning beds or self-tanner before or after.
  • Use gentle cleansers and moisturizers; aloe or cool compresses may soothe redness.
  • Avoid retinoids, glycolic acids, exfoliants, and harsh scrubs for several days.

Hair Shedding

  • Hair may appear to “grow” after treatment — this is normal shedding.
  • Shedding typically starts within 1–3 weeks.
  • Do not wax, tweeze, or thread between sessions.
  • Shaving is okay if needed.
