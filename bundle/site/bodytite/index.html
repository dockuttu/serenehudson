Fat reduction with skin tightening

BodyTite is a minimally-invasive body contouring procedure that uses radiofrequency energy to melt and remove fat while tightening the skin above it — addressing something traditional liposuction can’t: loose skin.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, BodyTite is performed by our physicians through tiny incisions under local anesthesia, targeting stubborn areas like the abdomen, flanks, arms, and thighs. Because it tightens as it reduces fat, results look smooth and refined. It involves some recovery and compression garments; candidacy is determined at consultation, and individual results vary.

What to Expect

Consultation

Your physician evaluates your areas and skin, reviews the procedure and recovery, and confirms candidacy.

Treatment

Under local anesthesia, radiofrequency energy melts and removes fat while tightening skin through tiny incisions.

Good to Know

BodyTite FAQ

It’s a minimally-invasive procedure done through tiny incisions under local anesthesia — less invasive than traditional surgery, but still a medical procedure with recovery.

Traditional liposuction only removes fat; BodyTite also tightens the skin using radiofrequency, which helps avoid the loose skin lipo can leave behind.

Expect swelling, bruising, and a compression garment for a period after treatment. Your physician will review your specific recovery.

You’ll notice contour changes early, with skin tightening and final results developing over several months.

BodyTite suits people with stubborn fat and mild-to-moderate skin laxity. Your physician will confirm at consultation.
