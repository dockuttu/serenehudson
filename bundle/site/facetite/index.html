The step between injectables and a facelift

Jowls, a softening jawline and a full or loose neck often don’t respond to filler or skin treatments alone. FaceTite works underneath the skin, where the laxity starts.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, FaceTite is performed by our physicians on the InMode IgniteRF platform, usually in one visit under local anesthesia. It’s often combined with Morpheus8 to improve the skin surface at the same time. For very small areas we use AccuTite, and QuantumRF is another option for the neck and jawline. Results develop over several months and vary from person to person.

The Experience

What to Expect

Consultation

An in-person exam with our physician to check skin quality, fat and laxity. You’ll get a written quote.

Treatment

Local anesthetic numbs the area. Treatment of the jawline and neck usually takes about an hour.

Results

Swelling and some bruising for about a week, and a chin strap for several days. Most people are back to work within a few days.

Maintenance

Tightening continues for six to twelve months. Most people need one treatment.

Good to Know

FaceTite FAQ

FaceTite starts at $3,500 at Serene Med Spa in Hudson. The exact price depends on the areas (for example jawline only or jawline and neck) and whether Morpheus8 is added, and is confirmed at your free consultation.

It’s a minimally invasive, in-office procedure. A few tiny openings (about the size of a needle stick) are made, and the area is numbed with local anesthetic. You’re awake, and there are no stitches in most cases. It is still a medical procedure with a short recovery.

No. IgniteRF uses radiofrequency, not a laser. A slim probe placed under the skin delivers energy to a precise, monitored temperature, which firms the tissue from underneath.

Our physicians perform every IgniteRF procedure, after an in-person evaluation to confirm you’re a good candidate.

Swelling and some bruising for about a week, with a chin strap for several days. Most people are back at work within a few days.

Some tightening is visible once swelling settles, and results keep improving for six to twelve months.

A facelift removes excess skin through longer incisions. FaceTite tightens from underneath through tiny openings, so it suits mild to moderate laxity. For very loose skin, surgery may be the better choice.
