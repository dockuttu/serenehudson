Lift and tighten — without a facelift

FaceTite offers powerful skin tightening for the lower face and neck through a tiny entry point, using radiofrequency to contract skin and melt small fat pockets — results that approach surgery, without the scars.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, FaceTite is performed by our physicians under local anesthesia to refine the jawline, jowls, and neck. It’s ideal for patients with mild-to-moderate laxity who want more than a non-invasive device can offer, but aren’t ready for a facelift. There’s some downtime; results develop over months, and individual results vary.
