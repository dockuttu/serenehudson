Why Medina patients make the drive to Hudson

Medina’s historic square is one of the nicest downtowns in Northeast Ohio, and Hudson’s is its closest cousin. Our patients from Medina, Brunswick, Wadsworth and Hinckley tell us the 35-minute drive is worth it for one reason: a physician they see every single visit.

Serene is owned by Dr. Shweta Arora (anesthesiology, aesthetic medicine) and Dr. Robin Arora (internal medicine, nephrology). There is no separate injector — the doctor who examines your face is the doctor who treats it. We’re an Allergan Platinum practice and an Alma and InMode provider, and since November 2026 the Hudson office also has the Sciton Halo, BBL and MOXI lasers, with Halo at a $1,199 launch price.

Every price is posted before you get in the car: Botox $10 per unit, Dysport $3.99 per unit, filler $500 per syringe, Morpheus8 $600 (three for $1,700), HydraFacial from $149, and physician-supervised weight loss at $149 per visit. See the full price list. Consultations are complimentary with no commitment, and because you’re driving a bit farther we make a point of treating at that first visit whenever you’re a candidate.

Two routes work. The fast one is Route 18 east to I-77, a short hop north to the Ohio Turnpike, east to the Route 8 exit, south to Route 303 and east into Hudson. The scenic one is Route 303 itself, which runs from Brunswick’s Center Road through Hinckley, Richfield and Peninsula straight to our front door. Many Medina patients stack two or three treatments in one visit, and weight-loss follow-ups can be done by telehealth so you aren’t driving every month.

Getting Here

From Medina to Serene Med Spa

From Medina (about 30 miles): take OH-18 East to I-77 North, then I-80 (Ohio Turnpike) East to Exit 180 for OH-8. Take OH-8 South to OH-303 (Streetsboro Rd), turn east and continue about 2 miles into downtown Hudson. We’re at 50 W Streetsboro St, Suite 2, just before the Route 91 / Main St light, with free parking outside. Allow about 35 minutes.

From Brunswick: OH-303 East (Center Rd) through Hinckley, Richfield and Peninsula directly into Hudson, about 35 minutes, or I-71 North to I-80 East. From Hinckley: OH-303 East, about 25 minutes. From Wadsworth: I-76 East to OH-8 North, then OH-303 East, about 40 minutes.
