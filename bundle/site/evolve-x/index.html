Three treatments on one hands-free platform

EvolveX treats the body in layers. Radiofrequency heat works on skin and fat, and electrical muscle stimulation works the muscle underneath, so one visit can target more than one concern.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, EvolveX treats the abdomen, flanks, thighs, buttocks and arms. The applicators strap in place, so you can relax, read or scroll while it works. Most people do a series of about six sessions, a week or two apart. It’s a strong fit after weight loss, including weight loss on a GLP-1 medication, when you want firmer skin and better muscle tone. Results build over the series and vary from person to person.

Tite, Tone or Transform

Which EvolveX treatment is right for you?

✦

Tite

Radiofrequency heat for loose or crepey skin. About 30–60 minutes.

✦

Tone

Electrical muscle stimulation to build and firm muscle. About 20–30 minutes.

✦

Transform

Radiofrequency plus muscle stimulation for thicker areas with stubborn fat. About an hour.

✦

Abdomen & Flanks

Our most-requested areas, for a firmer, tighter midsection.

✦

Thighs, Buttocks & Arms

Firm and tone the areas that are hardest to change with exercise alone.

✦

After Weight Loss

Tighten skin and support muscle tone as your body changes, including on a GLP-1 medication.

The Experience

What to Expect

Consultation

Your provider looks at your skin, muscle and target areas and chooses Tite, Tone, Transform or a combination.

Treatment

Applicators strap onto the area and run hands-free for 20 to 60 minutes. You feel warmth, and with Tone, strong muscle contractions.

Results

Skin firms and muscle tone builds over the weeks after your series. Results vary.

Maintenance

Most people do about six sessions, weekly or every other week, then an occasional maintenance session.
