Why NAD+ matters as we age

NAD+ is a molecule every cell uses to make energy and keep itself healthy, and NAD+ levels naturally decline with age. Our longevity menu is built around helping support healthy NAD+ levels.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, you can choose Niagen® (nicotinamide riboside, one of the most-researched NAD+ precursors) or NAD+ itself, as an IV infusion or a quick injection. Niagen IV infusions typically take about an hour, compared with the longer infusion times usually needed for NAD+. Every plan starts with a medical screening, and these treatments support wellness and healthy aging; they are not a treatment for any disease.

Good to Know

Longevity & NAD+ FAQ

NAD+ is a molecule your cells need to produce energy and stay healthy. Levels naturally decline with age, which is why supporting healthy NAD+ levels is a focus of longevity care.

Niagen® is nicotinamide riboside, a form of vitamin B3 your body uses to make NAD+. It is one of the most-researched NAD+ precursors, and Niagen IV infusions typically take about an hour, while NAD+ infusions are usually given more slowly over a longer visit.

Niagen IV is $499 for 500mg or $299 for 250mg. Niagen injections are $55 each or 10 for $499. NAD+ IV is $199 for 250mg or $299 for 500mg, NAD+ injections (50mg) are $35, and our longevity and glow drips are $149. Add-on glutathione is $35, and CoQ10 or vitamin D3 injections are $25.

Niagen and NAD+ are not recommended if you have active cancer or a history of cancer, or are pregnant or breastfeeding. Niagen is also not recommended with rheumatoid arthritis, and NAD+ is not recommended with uncontrolled heart disease. Please tell us if you take diabetes medications, blood thinners, blood pressure medications or opioids. We screen every patient before treatment.

Sermorelin is a peptide that signals your pituitary gland to release more of your own growth hormone. It is prescribed after a consultation as your own prescription, shipped to your home by the pharmacy, is not recommended with active cancer or during pregnancy, and is priced as a monthly program: $199 for injections, $249 for tablets, or $279 combined with NAD+.

Our Niagen®, NAD+ and sermorelin treatments are prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy. Compounded products are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or effectiveness, and results vary. They support general wellness and are not a treatment for any disease.

It depends on your goals. Common plans are a weekly Niagen IV or a Niagen injection every few days as a series. We’ll recommend a schedule at your visit.
