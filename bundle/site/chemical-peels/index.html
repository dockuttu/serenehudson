Resurface, brighten, renew

A chemical peel gently exfoliates the damaged surface layers of the skin, prompting fresh, healthy skin to take their place — for a brighter, smoother, more even complexion.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, we offer medical-grade peels including the VI Peel and The Perfect Derma Peel, chosen and customized to your skin and goals. Peels can target dullness, uneven tone, sun damage, fine lines, and breakouts. Your treatment is physician-supervised, and we’ll guide you on what to expect; individual results vary.

After Your Visit

Aftercare

Proper aftercare helps support healing, protect your skin, and enhance your results.

What to Expect

  • Mild redness, tightness, or sensitivity is normal.
  • You may experience peeling or flaking.
  • Symptoms typically improve within a few days to one week.

First 24–72 Hours

  • Keep skin clean and dry.
  • Use only gentle cleanser and moisturizer.
  • Avoid touching, picking, or peeling.
  • Stay hydrated; resume normal activities as tolerated.

Skin Care & Sun

  • Use a gentle cleanser and soothing moisturizer.
  • Avoid actives (retinol, acids, exfoliants) for 3–5 days.
  • Wear SPF 30+ daily; avoid sun and heat for 3–7 days.
  • No tanning beds or self-tanner.
