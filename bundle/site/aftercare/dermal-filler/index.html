After Your Visit

Filler Aftercare

Love your results, protect your investment.

First 24 Hours

  • Avoid touching, rubbing, or massaging the area.
  • Apply ice packs for swelling (10 minutes on / 10 off).
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Sleep with your head elevated.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise.
  • Avoid alcohol and excessive salt.
  • Avoid excessive heat — saunas, hot tubs, steam rooms, tanning beds.

First 48 Hours

  • Avoid facials, massage, microneedling, chemical peels, and laser treatments.
  • Avoid pressure on the area: face-down sleeping, tight goggles, excessive facial massage.

Lips Specifically

  • Expect more swelling the next morning.
  • Use a straw normally for the first day.
  • Small lumps are often swelling and usually soften over 1–2 weeks.

What is normal

SwellingBruisingTendernessFirmnessMild itching

⚠ Call your provider if you experience

Severe or increasing painSkin turning white, dusky or mottledVision changesSigns of infection

Results: Most swelling improves in 2–5 days.
