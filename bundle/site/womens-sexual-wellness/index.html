Comfortable, confidential, physician-led

Intimate wellness concerns — from comfort and confidence to changes after childbirth or menopause — are common and deserve real, discreet medical care. We offer non-surgical options that support women’s intimate wellness in a private setting.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, women’s sexual wellness options include V-Renew PRP — our physician-administered platelet-rich plasma (PRP) treatment, also known as the O-Shot® — along with VTone, FormaV, and Morpheus8 V, selected after a private consultation. Our board-certified physicians will discuss your goals and whether these options are appropriate for you. These treatments aim to support wellness; candidacy and results are individual and determined with your physician.
