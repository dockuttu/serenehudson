Hormones & Medical-Grade Skincare

Regenerate the tissue behind performance

Erectile changes with age are largely vascular: less blood flow, less responsive tissue. PRP delivers a concentrated dose of your own growth factors to stimulate new micro-vessels and repair tissue, improving firmness, sensation and, for some men, Peyronie’s-related curvature.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, P-Renew is performed by our physicians in a private room. A small blood draw is processed to concentrate platelets; after thorough numbing, PRP is injected at several points along the shaft — most men feel pressure, not pain. The visit takes about 45 minutes; expect mild swelling for a day and abstain from sexual activity for two to three days. Improvement in firmness and sensation typically appears over three to six weeks and continues for three months as tissue remodels. P-Renew is $1,200. We frequently combine it with Alma Duo shockwave therapy, which prepares the tissue and extends results, and with testosterone therapy when labs show low T. Individual results vary; P-Renew is not a substitute for cardiovascular evaluation of new erectile dysfunction.

The Experience

What to Expect

Consultation

A private physician consultation to review history, medications and cardiovascular risk and confirm P-Renew is appropriate.

Treatment

A blood draw, PRP preparation, thorough numbing, then several small injections; about 45 minutes.

Results

Mild swelling for a day; abstain 2–3 days. Firmness and sensation improve over 3–6 weeks and build for three months.

Maintenance

Results typically last 12–18 months; many men repeat annually or combine with Alma Duo.
