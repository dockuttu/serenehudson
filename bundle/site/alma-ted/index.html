Our Technology

Have you met TED?

Alma TED (TransEpidermal Delivery) is a non-invasive hair-restoration treatment for anyone with thinning hair or hair loss. Ultrasound sound waves and air pressure gently open the scalp and boost circulation, and a patented 33-ingredient Hair Care Formula is pushed straight into the follicle — no injections, no trauma, no post-treatment shedding.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, TED is the comfortable alternative for people who want the results of an in-office hair treatment without the needles of PRP or the shedding phase of other therapies. A session takes about 20 minutes; you’ll feel warmth and hear a ringing tone, then head back to your day with clean hair (just don’t wash it for 24 hours). Most patients see change within a month of the first session; three monthly treatments, then maintenance every few months, is the usual plan. TED also pairs well with PRP for a combined protocol. Individual results vary.

The Experience

What to Expect

Consultation

We review your hair-loss pattern and history, photograph baseline zones, and map a treatment plan (up to four zones for men, five for women).

Treatment

Arrive with clean, product-free hair. Each zone is primed with the ultrasound handpiece, the TED+ formula is applied, and the energy drives it in — about 20 minutes total.

Results

No downtime; skip washing for 24 hours. New growth typically shows within a month, with continued thickening over the series.

Maintenance

Three monthly sessions, then a maintenance treatment every 3–6 months to hold your results. Home-care formula is available.

How It Works

Ultrasound + TED+ Hair Care Formula

✦

1 · Acoustic Sound Waves

The TED handpiece delivers low-frequency ultrasound and air pressure that gently open the scalp’s outer layer and boost blood flow to the follicles.

✦

2 · TED+ Formula

A patented topical of 33 ingredients — peptides, growth factors, vitamins — is driven deep into the scalp to anchor the follicle, nourish growth, and prevent shedding.

✦

3 · 20 Minutes, No Needles

You feel a warm sensation and hear a ringing tone. No injections, no numbing, no pain, no shedding, and you go straight back to your day.

Most patients notice a difference within a month of the first session; a series of three monthly treatments is typical, with a maintenance session every few months.
