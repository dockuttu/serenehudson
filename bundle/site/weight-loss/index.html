Medical Weight Loss — Important Information

Prescription weight-management medications require a consultation with our licensed medical provider, who reviews your health history and determines whether a prescription is appropriate for you. When appropriate, FDA-approved medications such as Wegovy® (semaglutide) or Zepbound® (tirzepatide) are prescribed and sent to your pharmacy of choice; the medication is billed by your pharmacy and may be covered by insurance. Serene Med Spa charges for the medical visit only. These medications are not appropriate for everyone and individual results vary. Please review the full prescribing and safety information with your provider and pharmacist.
