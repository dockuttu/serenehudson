After Your Visit

Aftercare

Proper aftercare helps maintain hydration, protect your skin, and maximize your glow.

First 24–48 Hours

  • Expect skin to feel fresh, hydrated, and radiant.
  • Mild redness or tightness may occur temporarily.
  • Avoid touching or picking at the skin.
  • Keep skin hydrated with a gentle moisturizer.

Avoid 24–48 Hours

  • Strenuous exercise and excessive sweating.
  • Saunas, hot tubs, steam rooms, and swimming pools.
  • Heat and sweat can irritate freshly treated skin.

Skin Care & Sun

  • For several days, avoid retinoids/Retin-A, exfoliants, glycolic acids, scrubs, and harsh actives.
  • Avoid heavy makeup for 24 hours; mineral makeup preferred.
  • Avoid excessive sun and wear SPF 30+ daily.
