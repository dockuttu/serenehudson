Our Technology

Love laser? Try plasma.

Fractional skin resurfacing has been the gold standard for repairing aging skin for decades — but the laser version can mean days or weeks of recovery. Opus Plasma delivers the same fractional-resurfacing science with a different energy source, plasma, that precisely controls how long energy touches the tissue. Less inflammation, faster healing, same collagen-rebuilding result.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, Opus Plasma is our go-to for skin tone and texture, fine lines and wrinkles, acne scars, stretch marks, and the neck. The treatment takes about 15 minutes after topical numbing, and most patients see 24–48 hours of mild to moderate redness and swelling at moderate settings — makeup can go on after a day. You’ll notice improvement after one session; two to three treatments deliver the best results. Settings are matched to your Fitzpatrick skin type and individual results vary.

The Experience

What to Expect

Consultation

We assess your skin, concerns, and downtime tolerance and choose a gentle glow-up setting or a deeper resurfacing plan.

Treatment

Topical numbing for 30–45 minutes, then about 15 minutes of treatment. You’ll feel warmth; most patients tolerate it easily.

Results

24–48 hours of pinkness and mild swelling at moderate settings; makeup after a day. Skin looks fresher within a week and keeps improving for months as collagen rebuilds.

Maintenance

Two to three sessions about a month apart give the best result, then a maintenance treatment once or twice a year.

Plasma vs. Laser

Why We Reach for Opus Plasma

✦

Same Science, New Energy

Like fractional laser, Opus creates a grid of micro-injuries that trigger the skin to rebuild. The difference is the energy source: plasma, generated by radiofrequency, instead of light.

✦

Half the Downtime

Plasma controls how long energy touches tissue, so there is far less collateral inflammation. A published study found Opus needed about 50% less downtime than fractional laser resurfacing.

✦

Dialed to Your Skin

Energy goes up or down per pass, so the same device does a glow-up with a day of pinkness or a deeper resurfacing for etched lines and scars.

✦

Trouble Spots Welcome

Neck, around the mouth, around the eyes, and the hands — the delicate areas that make many lasers nervous are where Opus shines.

✦

15-Minute Treatment

Numbing cream goes on for about 30–45 minutes; the treatment itself is roughly 15 minutes for a full face.

✦

Pairs With Everything

Opus layers well with Botox, filler, Skinvive, and PRP for a complete, natural-looking refresh.
