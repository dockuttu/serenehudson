Learn from a working, physician-led practice

Aesthetics is equal parts medicine and artistry. Our training is built to give providers both — real technique, real anatomy, and real bedside judgment.

Serene Aesthetics Academy will bring providers into the same physician-led environment our patients trust every day. Rather than a weekend crash course, our goal is thorough, hands-on education with the safety fundamentals and mentorship that build genuinely confident injectors. Curriculum, dates, and enrollment details are being finalized now — add your name to the waitlist to be the first to know.
