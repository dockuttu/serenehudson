A measured, medical approach

The same hyaluronic acid fillers used for facial contouring can be placed evenly beneath the skin of the shaft to increase circumference. Done by a physician with the right product and technique, the result feels natural and functions normally.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, Grow Girth begins with a private physician consultation to set realistic expectations and confirm you are a candidate. On treatment day the area is numbed thoroughly; filler is placed in a layered, even pattern through a few entry points using a cannula, then molded smooth. The visit takes about 45 minutes. Expect mild swelling and tenderness for several days, and abstain from sexual activity for two weeks while the product integrates. Most men use two to four syringes at $800 each, adding roughly half an inch to an inch of circumference depending on volume. Results last 12–18 months and can be topped up; because it is hyaluronic acid, it can also be dissolved. Grow Girth is for girth only — it does not increase length or treat erectile function, which we address with P-Renew PRP and Alma Duo.
