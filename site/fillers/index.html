Volume and definition, thoughtfully restored

As we age, we naturally lose volume in the cheeks, lips, and around the mouth — which can make the face look tired or flat. Dermal fillers gently replace that volume, smooth folds, and add definition where you want it, using gel-like hyaluronic acid that your body recognizes.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, filler is an art form. Our board-certified physicians study proportion and balance to create results that look like a better-rested version of you — never overfilled. Every plan starts with a consultation to understand what you like and what you'd like to refine.
