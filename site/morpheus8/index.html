Firmer, smoother skin — without surgery

Morpheus8 pairs microneedling with radiofrequency energy to reach deeper layers of the skin, stimulating your body's own collagen and elastin. The result is gradual tightening, refined texture, and a healthier glow — on the face, neck, and body.

At Serene Med Spa in Hudson, Morpheus8 treatments are customized and physician-supervised. We tailor the depth and energy to your skin and goals, whether you're targeting early signs of aging, acne scarring, enlarged pores, or mild laxity along the jawline and neck.

The Experience

What to Expect

Consultation & Prep

We assess your skin and goals, then apply numbing cream for a comfortable treatment.

Treatment

The device delivers microneedling with radiofrequency at a customized depth. Sessions typically take 30–60 minutes.

Brief Recovery

Expect redness and minor swelling for a day or two, similar to a mild sunburn.

Progressive Results

Skin continues to improve over weeks to months as new collagen forms. A series of about three sessions is common.
